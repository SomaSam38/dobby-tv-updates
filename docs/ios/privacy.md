# Dobby TV privacy policy

Updated 31 October 2026 · iPhone and iPad

Dobby TV is developed by Sam Wysock-Wright. It is a media player for playlists and streams you supply; it does not provide television subscriptions or a media catalogue.

## What stays on your device

Dobby stores provider logins and playlist addresses in this device’s iOS Keychain. Saved catalogues, favourites, your watchlist, viewing progress and downloaded media are stored locally with iOS data protection. Catalogues and downloads may contain provider stream URLs or access tokens. The app’s catalogue and download directories are excluded from device backups; Keychain entries use device-only protection. Dobby does not operate an account, cloud library or analytics service, and has no advertising or tracking SDK.

## Connections you choose

Adding or refreshing a playlist, loading artwork and programme information, streaming and downloading connect directly to your provider and the servers it supplies. Those services receive the information needed to answer your request, including your IP address, the requested resource and any provider login or access token in the address. Their own privacy policies and retention practices apply; we do not operate those services or have access to their logs. Recognised legacy provider links may be resolved to the provider’s currently verified server. Dobby does not send your library or viewing history to the developer.

Use HTTPS when your provider supports it. HTTP provider connections are unencrypted. A valid HTTPS connection does not change your provider’s own data practices.

## AirPlay and optional Samsung sharing

AirPlay sends playback information to the receiving device. Stream addresses may include provider access tokens. Compatible MKV playback can serve a temporary stream to your selected receiver over your local network. Use receivers and networks you trust.

If you choose to pair with Dobby on a Samsung TV, playlist setup information, saved titles and viewing progress can be exchanged with that paired TV over your home network. This is optional. The app does not provide internet-based library synchronisation, and pairing does not send your library to the developer.

## Your controls

Remove downloaded items in Downloads. Clear viewing history in Settings. Removing a playlist in Settings removes its saved login, favourites and progress from this app. Remove playlists before uninstalling if you want to clear their Keychain entries; iOS may retain Keychain data after an app is deleted. Removing data in Dobby does not delete records held independently by your provider or a paired TV. You can unlink the TV in the shared-library settings.

## Support and Apple services

If you email us, we use your email address and the information you choose to send to respond and resolve your request. Please do not send passwords, private playlist links or access tokens. We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle the request and any applicable record-keeping obligations. You may ask us to delete correspondence we hold, subject to those obligations.

Apple handles App Store distribution and any diagnostics you choose to share through Apple. GitHub hosts these public support documents and may process website connection data under its own privacy policy. Opening an external link is subject to that destination’s privacy policy.
