# Dobby TV for iPhone and iPad — Support

Your playlists. Your downtime.

Dobby TV is a player for your own M3U playlist or Xtream-compatible provider. It does not include channels, movies, subscriptions or a Dobby account. Use only content you have permission to access and download.

## Add your library

Open Dobby, choose **Add your playlist**, select **M3U playlist** or **Xtream login**, then enter the details supplied by your provider. Give the playlist a name and tap **Add**. Large libraries can take time to import. Dobby connects directly to your provider.

## Find your way around

Use Home, Live TV, Movies, Series and My List. On iPhone, open the More menu for Settings and Downloads. iPad provides a sidebar. Save favourites and a watchlist, and resume supported videos from Continue Watching. Supported series include season and episode selection and optional automatic next-episode playback.

## Playback, AirPlay and Picture in Picture

Use the player controls to pause, seek and choose supported audio and subtitle tracks. AirPlay and Picture in Picture depend on the stream, codec, device and receiver. Compatible MKV movies can use native Apple playback through on-device repackaging; other formats may use the compatibility player. For local MKV AirPlay, keep the sending device and receiver on the same reachable Wi-Fi network. Some providers, formats or DRM restrictions can prevent playback.

## Offline downloads

Download supported movie files, episodes or seasons over Wi-Fi when your provider permits downloading. Adaptive HLS offline downloads are not currently supported. Downloads remain on the device where they were saved and can be removed from Downloads.

## Sharing with Samsung TV

Optional Shared Samsung library pairing shares saved titles and viewing progress for a matching supported Xtream account. The paired Samsung TV must run a compatible Dobby build and be reachable on the same home network. Internet/cloud library sync is not included.

## Connection problems

Check the provider address and that your subscription is active. Update Dobby, check automatic Date & Time, then retry. Do not turn off certificate verification. If a secure connection fails, copy the error’s diagnostic text, including the server name and request stage. Contact your provider about account and server problems. For Dobby support, tell us your device model, iOS/iPadOS version, Dobby build and what you were doing. **Do not send passwords or complete private playlist URLs.**
